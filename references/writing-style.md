# Writing Style for Research Learning

## Desired style

- Clear, patient, technically accurate Chinese by default when output language is zh-CN.
- Explain specialist terms when they first become necessary.
- Use concrete examples to bridge intuition and mathematics.
- Prefer concise paragraphs and informative subheadings.
- Keep the paper's original terminology and notation.
- Use tables for comparisons and execution traces when they improve clarity.

## Avoid

- acronym-heavy introductions;
- long lists of module names without causal relationships;
- formula dumps without interpretation;
- empty phrases such as “显著提升了性能” without specifying what, where, and by how much;
- unsupported claims about novelty or superiority;
- overlong background sections that crowd out the method.

## Reader check

At the end of each central method subsection, include a short “一句话理解” that accurately captures the mechanism without replacing the technical explanation.
